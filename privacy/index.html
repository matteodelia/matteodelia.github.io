STATISTICHE

Per capire quali lavori interessano di più il sito ha un sistema di statistiche proprio, senza cookie, ospitato su Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., Stati Uniti). Conta in forma anonima le visite, i progetti guardati e per quanto tempo, i film aperti e quanto vengono guardati, i link cliccati, il sito da cui arrivi, il tipo di dispositivo e browser e la città approssimativa.

L'indirizzo IP non viene salvato: serve a Cloudflare per ricavare la posizione approssimativa e, insieme ai dati del browser e a una chiave segreta che cambia ogni mese, a calcolare un codice anonimo da cui non si può risalire a te. I totali vengono copiati in un archivio privato del titolare.

Base giuridica: legittimo interesse a capire come viene visto il portfolio (art. 6.1.f GDPR). Informativa di Cloudflare

VIDEO

The films are hosted on Vimeo (Vimeo.com, Inc., United States). When you open the site the Vimeo player loads: it receives your IP address and browser data and may set its own cookies, including analytics cookies used to count views.

Vimeo privacy policy · Vimeo cookies

ANALYTICS

To understand which works people care about most, the site has its own cookieless analytics, hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., United States). It counts, anonymously, visits, which projects are viewed and for how long, which films are opened and how much of them is watched, clicked links, the site you came from, your device and browser type and your approximate city.

Your IP address is not stored: Cloudflare uses it to derive the approximate location and, together with browser data and a secret key that changes every month, to compute an anonymous code that cannot be traced back to you. The totals are copied into the controller's private archive.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding how the portfolio is viewed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare privacy policy

COOKIES

The site sets no cookies of its own, and neither does its analytics. The only ones are Vimeo's, described above. You can block or delete them in your browser settings: the videos keep working.
